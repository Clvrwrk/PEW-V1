## The Official Roofing Partner of Texas Motor Speedway

Pro Exteriors LLC is the **Official Roofing Partner of Texas Motor Speedway** — one of the largest and most recognizable motorsports venues in North America, located in Fort Worth, Texas.

This is not a one-time project. It is a long-term, institutional partnership structured as a **5-year contract** covering ongoing multi-phase roof replacement, preventative maintenance, and roof asset management across the speedway's extensive facility footprint.

## The Challenge

Texas Motor Speedway's infrastructure spans grandstands, suites, pit lane facilities, and support structures across a massive footprint — each with distinct roofing systems, exposure profiles, and operational constraints. The venue hosts major events on a fixed calendar, which means roofing work must be sequenced around event windows and executed without disrupting operations.

The scope required a contractor with:
- Institutional-level project management discipline
- Multi-phase capital planning capability
- Emergency and preventative maintenance infrastructure
- The organizational structure to coordinate across multiple buildings and timelines simultaneously

## Our Approach

Pro Exteriors deployed its Commercial Division and Service & Asset Management Division to manage the engagement across both phases: active replacement work and ongoing maintenance.

Each phase of the project is documented in detail — condition reports, photographic records, scope-of-work completions, and warranty registrations. All documentation is structured to support Pro Exteriors' ongoing project, which will capture the full scope of the partnership over the contract term.

## A Story Worth Telling

The Texas Motor Speedway engagement is actively being documented across all phases as the foundation for a **future documentary** on commercial roofing at institutional scale. The documentation captures what it actually takes to protect a facility of this size and complexity over a multi-year period — the planning, the sequencing, the teams, and the outcomes.

## What It Represents

Texas Motor Speedway is the clearest signal of the kind of contractor Pro Exteriors has become: one that institutional clients trust with long-term asset protection, not just a single repair call.
