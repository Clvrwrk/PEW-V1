## What commercial roof repair includes

Membrane patches on [TPO](/commercial-roofing/tpo/) and [EPDM](/commercial-roofing/epdm/) use weld or splice materials from Carlisle, Versico, and Duro-Last so chemistry matches the field sheet. Modified bitumen and built-up repairs pull compatible Soprema and Atlas products. Metal repairs include panel alignment, fastener replacement, and sealant protocols for McElroy and Sentrigard systems we maintain in regional inventory.

Flashing and penetrations remain the highest-frequency repair surface area — HVAC curbs, drains, parapets, and pipe boots. We probe-test welds on thermoplastics and document every curb before returning the roof to service. When infrared is in scope, wet insulation is marked out so you do not pay for patches over saturated substrate.

For low-slope inventory management, tie repair decisions to [flat roof repair](/commercial-roofing/flat-roof-repair/) methodology and, when appropriate, [commercial roof coating](/commercial-roofing/roof-coatings/) restoration before capital replacement.

## Repair versus coat versus replace

The median reroof proposal in the index is about **34×** a typical scoped repair and about **110×** a typical leak call — so the commercial roofing decision is rarely “patch or tear-off.” It is usually **leak triage → scoped repair → overlay or coating → replacement**.

| Stage | When it fits | Indexed median to cite |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Leak triage | Active water, single breach, dry insulation nearby | **$907** leak call |
| Scoped repair | Wet insulation localized, failed seams or curbs | **$2,907** scoped job |
| Restoration coating | Dry substrate, adhesion pass, owner needs years not decades | **$30,000** quoted coating scope (prep + topcoat) |
| Re-cover or overlay | Deck sound, limited moisture, major seams rebuilt | AccuLynx sold repairs median **$5,250**; overlays case-by-case |
| Replacement | Moisture widespread, deck compromised | Proposal median **$99,850** |

Texas Motor Speedway illustrates repair-first sequencing: April 2025 EPDM leak work across five occupied buildings, then building-by-building overlays and a main-office TPO replacement — not a campus-wide tear-off. Approved job value across eight sold scopes totals **$1,454,345.12** with overlays on media center, storage, and concessions and coating scopes on smaller stands.

## Storm context without guessing damage

DFW NOAA aggregates (2016–2026) show **1,409** hail reports within the metro radius, **208** at two inches or larger, and a **5.25-inch** reported stone in Collin County on 2016-04-11. Atlanta shows **134** hail reports with only **2** at two inches or larger in the same window but **1,102** severe-wind reports — *Source: hail_by_metro.md, NOAA Storm Events and SPC, built 2026-11-19.*

Post-event commercial roof inspection should separate cosmetic from structural before you commit capital. Pair storm documentation with [commercial roof leak repair](/commercial-roofing/leak-repair/) when interiors are wet.

## Commercial roof inspection and maintenance hooks

Indexed inspection tickets median **$600** billed (n=34). Inspection is the documented on-ramp to scoped repair, coating suitability, or honest replace math. Bi-annual drain walks and seam probes reduce repeat leak tickets: **67** indexed properties logged two or more leak calls in the 14-month Centerpoint window — repeat work is visible in the data even when owners feel they “just fixed it last year.”

## Industrial and low-slope inventory

Industrial roofing repair on warehouses and production plants usually means wide TPO fields, RTU-heavy curbs, and edge metal exposed to forklift traffic. Retail and medical pads mix mod-bit aging with EPDM additions. Metal low-slope sections tie to [metal](/commercial-roofing/metal/) protocols when panels, not membrane, carry the failure. One commercial roof repair program should document each roof zone by system so procurement is not comparing a $900 leak on Building C to a $30,000 coating scope on Building A without context.
