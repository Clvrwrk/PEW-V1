Facility managers call when ceiling tile is wet, inventory is threatened, or a surgical suite cannot tolerate downtime — the job is to stop interior risk, find the breach on the deck, and produce a permanent scope priced against real ticket history.

## How commercial leaks actually behave

Water rarely drips straight down from the membrane puncture. It tracks along deck seams, fills flute valleys on metal, runs inside insulation until it finds a path through a penetration, and emerges twenty feet from the stain. Leak repair that only patches above the interior drip usually buys a recall. Our workflow maps the path: interior evidence, roof zone walk, core or infrared when insulation is suspect, then permanent repair on the actual failure.

Membrane type changes method: thermoplastic patches weld; EPDM splices; mod-bit ties into compatible asphaltic repair. Cross-reference [TPO roofing systems](/commercial-roofing/tpo/) and [EPDM roof repair](/commercial-roofing/epdm/) for material-specific protocols after triage.

## Common leak failure modes

**Storm-driven membrane and metal damage.** Hail bruising, wind-peeled edges, and debris punctures spike demand after spring events. DFW sits in a hail-dense corridor — **1,609** NOAA-reported hail events across core counties (2016–2026), maximum reported size **5.90 in** — *Source: hail_by_metro.md / NOAA Storm Events via Supabase `zcta_hail_summary`, read 2026-11-19.* Atlanta records **97** events in the same window, max **2.50 in** — same source. Storm documentation supports commercial carriers without inflating scope. City context: [commercial roofing Dallas](/commercial-roofing/dallas/) and [commercial roofing Atlanta](/commercial-roofing/atlanta/).

**Deferred seam and flashing maintenance.** Open TPO welds, aged EPDM tape, and failed pitch pockets leak on the first sustained rain after a dry spell — classic “it was fine yesterday” calls.

**Mechanical and trade damage.** HVAC service, solar installs, and signage anchors compromise flashings. We repair to manufacturer detail, not tar blobs.

**Drain and scupper failure.** Overflows mimic membrane leaks. [Flat roof repair](/commercial-roofing/flat-roof-repair/) drainage work often pairs with leak tickets.

**Interior misdirection.** Condensation, HVAC duct sweating, and sprinkler breaks masquerade as roof leaks. We rule out non-roof sources before membrane work.

## Containment, documentation, and permanent scope

Containment protects assets while the roof investigation runs: interior protection where needed, stakeholder notification, and roof access coordinated with operations. Photography is timestamped when storm or insurance files are likely.

Permanent scope lists materials, line items, and schedule — or escalation to scoped [flat roof repair](/commercial-roofing/flat-roof-repair/) and [commercial roof coating](/commercial-roofing/roof-coatings/) when the assembly can be restored instead of patched forever. We do not publish 2–4 hour or other fixed response-time marketing claims on this site; regional dispatch runs through **844-336-PROS** and local offices without verbal SLA theater in content.

## When leak triage escalates

Repeat leak tickets on the same zone within twelve months signal systemic failure — wet insulation, chronic ponding, or incompatible prior patches. We flag escalation in writing so capital planning can align with [commercial roof repair](/commercial-roofing/repair/) scoped medians (**$2,603.65** national) rather than chasing **$902.50** leak medians indefinitely.
