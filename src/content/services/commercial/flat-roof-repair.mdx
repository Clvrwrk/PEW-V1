Flat roof repair keeps logistics, retail, and healthcare properties operating while capital plans catch up. Low-slope commercial roofs do not leak because gravity helps water leave quickly — they leak because water sits, finds the weakest detail, and soaks insulation until deck loads and interior finishes pay the price. Pro Exteriors scopes flat roof repair as assembly work: membrane, insulation, drainage, and metal, not a single patch rectangle.

Pair this service with city pages when storm or climate context matters: [commercial roofing Dallas](/commercial-roofing/dallas/) for hail-dense procurement and [commercial roofing Atlanta](/commercial-roofing/atlanta/) for humidity-driven EPDM maintenance.

## Common low-slope failure modes

**Ponding and drainage.** Clogged drains, crushed scuppers, and insufficient slope create standing water that accelerates membrane fatigue and ice damming in fringe freeze markets. Many [commercial roof leak repair](/commercial-roofing/leak-repair/) calls trace to hydraulics, not holes in the field.

**Open seams and failed laps.** TPO and PVC demand probe-tested welds; EPDM demands splice discipline. Deferred maintenance turns a $900-class leak into a $2,600-class scoped repair when wet insulation spreads.

**Penetrations and equipment curbs.** RTU curbs, grease vents, and conduit boots concentrate movement. HVAC contractors who disconnect flashings without reinstalling manufacturer details create repeat tickets — we document curb condition on every visit.

**Insulation and vapor drive.** Wet polyiso loses R-value and crushes under foot traffic. Infrared mapping defines whether flat roof repair stays patch-economical or whether [commercial roof repair](/commercial-roofing/repair/) must escalate to recover or [replacement](/commercial-roofing/replacement/).

**Edge metal and coping.** Wind events peel coping and expose fascia. Post-storm inspection matters in hail corridors: NOAA Storm Events data for core DFW counties records **1,609** hail events (2016–2026) with max **5.90 in** reported hail — *Source: hail_by_metro.md / NOAA via Supabase `zcta_hail_summary`, read 2026-11-19.* Atlanta metro shows **97** events, max **2.50 in** — same source — with wind debris still driving edge failure.

**Built-up and mod-bit fatigue.** Alligatoring, ridge splits, and failed flood coats on aging asphaltic systems need compatible manufacturers — not random mastic buckets.

## Membrane-specific repair paths

**TPO and PVC.** Heat-welded patches, strip-ins at seams, and curb rebuilds per [TPO roofing systems](/commercial-roofing/tpo/). We probe representative welds after multi-curb projects.

**EPDM.** Splice tapes, primers, and peel-and-stick per [EPDM roof repair](/commercial-roofing/epdm/). Humidity markets demand dry-window scheduling for adhesive work.

**Coating restoration.** When moisture is absent and adhesion tests pass, [commercial roof coating](/commercial-roofing/roof-coatings/) extends mod-bit, EPDM, TPO, and qualifying metal decks after prep.

**New system planning.** When repair cost-per-year fails, bridge to [flat roof systems](/commercial-roofing/flat-roof-systems/) install specs.

## Documentation owners should expect

Every flat roof repair closeout should give facilities a map: where water entered, what insulation was removed, what materials were used, and what maintenance interval applies. Photo sets tie to roof zones so the next technician does not reinterpret stains. Insurance and risk teams get timestamped storm documentation when events trigger inspection — without inventing damage beyond what the roof shows.

Active leaks on flat roofs route through **844-336-PROS**; closeout includes roof-zone photos and insulation treatment notes facilities can audit.

## Buyer checklist before signing flat roof repair

Confirm the scope names membrane type, insulation treatment, drain work, and warranty impact. Ask whether infrared was used and whether wet areas were cut out or marked for future monitoring. Require photo closeout tied to roof zones. Benchmark line items against Repair Index medians on the [commercial roof repair](/commercial-roofing/repair/) pillar — then judge whether your building’s complexity justifies above- or below-median pricing.

Portfolio managers standardizing vendors across DFW and Atlanta should align detail standards on [commercial roofing Dallas](/commercial-roofing/dallas/) and [commercial roofing Atlanta](/commercial-roofing/atlanta/) so the same curb rebuild looks the same on both campuses.
