TPO roofing systems from Pro Exteriors serve national portfolios with the same weld standards whether the property is in Denver, Atlanta, or Dallas. Procurement teams choose TPO when they need welded seams, reflective surface performance, and installed cost that sits between EPDM and PVC without surrendering field-serviceability. Install work is only half the lifecycle — the other half is honest repair scope when heat, foot traffic, or hail stresses an aging membrane.

## TPO vs EPDM vs PVC

| Spec | TPO | EPDM | PVC |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seam method | Hot-air weld | Tape / splice | Hot-air weld |
| Reflectivity | High (white) | Low (black default) | High |
| Typical use | General commercial | Cold / ballast | Chemical exposure |
| Repair entry | [TPO page](/commercial-roofing/tpo/) | [EPDM roof repair](/commercial-roofing/epdm/) | Repair pillar + manufacturer |

Thermoplastic chemistry matters on patches: we match weld rod and sheet formulation so cold welds do not embrittle in a Texas summer or peel after an Atlanta humidity cycle. When an owner compares rubber to TPO, we send them to [EPDM roof repair](/commercial-roofing/epdm/) for splice economics and keep TPO discussions on probe-tested seam work.

## Common TPO failure modes

TPO fails in predictable places when attachment, detailing, or maintenance slip — not randomly across the field.

**Open field seams and weld voids.** Hot-air welds that were under-temp, contaminated, or never probe-tested fail first under wind uplift and thermal movement. On service calls we cutter-probe suspect seams before recommending a blanket recover.

**Flashings at curbs, drains, and parapets.** HVAC maintenance traffic concentrates punctures and open terminations at equipment curbs. Drain sumps with standing water accelerate membrane fatigue at clamping rings. These details drive a disproportionate share of [commercial roof leak repair](/commercial-roofing/leak-repair/) dispatches.

**Puncture and fastener back-out.** Mechanically attached systems can show fastener back-out or washer cutting through scrim when coverboard is thin or foot traffic is heavy. Fully adhered fields fail from dropped tools and unreinforced walkway omissions.

**Hail and impact bruising.** Reflective white TPO hides impact damage until IR or storm inspection maps soft spots. In hail-dense markets we document bruising before agreeing to patch-only economics. NOAA Storm Events data aggregated for Dallas shows **1,609** hail events across core DFW counties (2016–2026) with up to **5.90-inch** reported hail — *Source: hail_by_metro.md / NOAA Storm Events via Supabase `zcta_hail_summary`, read 2026-11-19.*

**Aged membrane and coating mismatch.** Surface chalking on older TPO can reject coating without proper prep. When adhesion tests pass, [commercial roof coating](/commercial-roofing/roof-coatings/) buys years; when they fail, we scope membrane repair through [flat roof repair](/commercial-roofing/flat-roof-repair/) instead of painting over a tired sheet.

## Install and recover quality gates

New TPO and recover projects gate on substrate dryness, FM or UL uplift compliance where specified, and manufacturer warranty filing when the owner needs it. Pre-install moisture scans prevent welding over wet coverboard. Edge metal and coping details are treated as structural wind accessories, not trim. Closeout includes probe logs on representative seam samples, penetration photo sets, and warranty registration when the manufacturer program applies.

Recover decisions pair with [commercial roof repair](/commercial-roofing/repair/) economics: when less than roughly a quarter of insulation reads wet and the deck is sound, recover plus targeted strip-in repair often beats tear-off disruption.
