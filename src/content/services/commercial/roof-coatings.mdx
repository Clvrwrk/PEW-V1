Commercial roof coating extends service life when repairs and diagnostics say the assembly beneath the membrane is dry and structurally sound. Use this page with [flat roof repair](/commercial-roofing/flat-roof-repair/) and the repair pillar for total program design.

## When coating beats tear-off

Coating wins when infrared shows limited moisture, seams can be rebuilt, and the owner needs another 7–15 years without a capital event. Coating loses when insulation is saturated or decks are compromised — then [commercial roof repair](/commercial-roofing/repair/) documents replacement economics.
