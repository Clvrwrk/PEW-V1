## Common failure modes on DFW commercial roofs

**Hail impact and follow-on leakage.** Thermoplastic bruising and EPDM soft spots may not leak until the next storm cycle. Post-event inspection separates cosmetic from structural damage before carriers close files.

**Heat and UV on white TPO.** Reflective membranes still age at seams and curbs. Surface erosion can reject coating without prep — see [commercial roof coating](/commercial-roofing/roof-coatings/) after adhesion testing.

**Wind uplift at edges and large panels.** Texas wind codes push coping and edge metal specs. Peeler details show up as mystery interior stains until someone walks the parapet.

**RTU curb fatigue.** DFW’s flat roof stock runs heavy HVAC. Maintenance traffic without walkway discipline punctures fields and rocks flashings.

**Drain ponding on older builds.** Negative slope and clogged drains create chronic leak tickets that [flat roof repair](/commercial-roofing/flat-roof-repair/) corrects structurally, not cosmetically.
