## Common failure modes in Atlanta’s commercial stock

**EPDM seam and adhesive fatigue.** Heat and humidity accelerate tape and splice failure faster than inland cold markets. Curbs and penetrations on older rubber dominate service volume — the pattern behind our anonymized multi-building EPDM program in frontmatter case work.

**Mod-bit and built-up ponding.** Aging asphaltic systems on pre-1980s low-slope decks hold water at drains and scuppers. Repair requires compatible flood coats and drainage correction through [flat roof repair](/commercial-roofing/flat-roof-repair/).

**TPO weld voids on newer logistics.** Alpharetta and south metro distribution centers added thermoplastic inventory in the last decade. Weld QC at install determines whether owners live on [TPO roofing systems](/commercial-roofing/tpo/) repair or premature recover.

**Wind-driven edge metal.** Hail is sparse relative to Dallas — **97** NOAA-reported events across Atlanta-metro counties (2016–2026), max **2.50 in** — *Source: hail_by_metro.md / NOAA Storm Events via Supabase `zcta_hail_summary`, read 2026-11-19.* Wind still peels coping and throws debris punctures; post-storm walks matter.

**Condensation misreads.** Humid summers push interior moisture complaints that mimic roof leaks. Diagnosis rules out HVAC and envelope sources before membrane patches.
