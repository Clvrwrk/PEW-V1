Pro Exteriors serves the Florida Panhandle from our Pensacola office at 545 Brent Lane #141 — a local base for commercial and residential roofing across Escambia and Santa Rosa counties.

Northwest Florida brings a distinct mix of Gulf Coast humidity, tropical-storm and hurricane wind exposure, and fast-moving thunderstorm damage. The Pensacola team handles commercial roof assessments, residential inspections, emergency leak triage, repair, and replacement using the same documented inspection standards Pro Exteriors applies in every market: written scope, photo evidence, and clear next steps before work begins.

**Service area:** Pensacola, Milton, Pace, Gulf Breeze, Navarre, Cantonment, and surrounding communities in Escambia and Santa Rosa counties.

For commercial properties, we support low-slope membrane systems, metal roofing, repair, and replacement. For homeowners, we prioritize asphalt shingle replacement, storm documentation, and emergency response when active water intrusion threatens the interior.

Start with a [commercial RFQ](/contact/commercial/) or [residential inspection request](/contact/residential/inspection/) and include the property address, roof type, active leak status, and any insurance timing constraints. For active storm damage, call [844-336-PROS](tel:+18443367767) — do not wait on a form.
