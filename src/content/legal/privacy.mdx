# Privacy Policy

This notice explains how Pro Exteriors handles the information you share with us through this website.

## Information we collect

When you fill out a form, request an inspection or call us from this site, we collect the details you choose to give us, such as your name, phone number, email address, property address and a description of your project. We also use the analytics and functional cookies described in our [Cookies Policy](/cookies/).

## How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, schedule and carry out work on your property, and keep you updated about that work. We do not sell your personal information.

## Contact

To ask what information we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it, email [Office@proexteriorsus.com](mailto:Office@proexteriorsus.com) or call [844-336-PROS](tel:+18443367767).
